# Visual and Motion Plan

**Creative principle:** no static chapter-card slideshow. Build an original, story-led sequence from procedural 3D scenes, animated maps, live data labels and kinetic typography. No generated scene may be presented as documentary evidence of a real attack, vessel or military event.

## Long-form master

- Target: 7:30–8:30, 1920×1080, 24 fps, 16:9.
- Minimum craft intent: at least 8 distinct visual assets, 5 genuinely different moving scene setups, meaningful camera movement throughout and no repeated loop used as filler.
- Composition: restrained black/charcoal background, off-white type, one restrained signal colour for risk/flow; high-contrast Devanagari captions.
- No unlicensed footage, no stock imagery, no copyrighted music. Use original Blender geometry and data graphics. If no rights-cleared sound bed is available, narration plus subtle original procedural sound design is preferable to unlicensed music.

## Asset and rights plan

- Original Blender meshes, coastlines and route geometry: create in-house; preserve .blend and Python build script.
- Data charts: generate from the cited EIA/PPAC figures; include source/date/units on screen.
- Font: use a Devanagari font only after confirming the exact installed file and its open licence/notice.
- Narration: Bol TTS Marathi is the documented DOCS local candidate (Apache-2.0 model/code declaration), but exact voicepack/dataset terms, cache presence, output quality and human Marathi audition remain unverified. Do not synthesize on an unverified runner.
- Music: none selected. Do not add copyrighted or unlicensed music.
- Foley/ambience: original procedural sound only, with source and licence recorded.
- Do not use Reuters photos/video or other editorial imagery without a separate valid licence. Reuters is a factual text source here, not a media asset.
