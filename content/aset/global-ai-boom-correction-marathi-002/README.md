# ASET Documentary 002 — AI Boom, Market Correction आणि पुढचा धोका?

**Language:** Marathi (Devanagari)  
**Format:** YouTube long-form, 16:9, target runtime 10 minutes  
**Editorial date:** 22 November 2026  
**Status:** Production source package; render/publish pending  
**Scope:** Global equities, AI infrastructure investment, credit links, market risk and Indian investor context.

## Editorial promise
A fact-led explainer, not a crash prediction. It separates a market correction from a bear market, explains the AI infrastructure financing chain, and shows why a real technology revolution can still produce bad investments.

- `render.py`: creates narration, scene cards, captions, MP4 and thumbnail

## Production notes
The workflow uses Google Translate's public gTTS endpoint for Marathi narration, Pillow for original graphic cards, and FFmpeg for assembly. This is a no-key, no-paid-credit route, but gTTS is an unofficial public endpoint and voice quality must be reviewed. Visuals are original typography/data cards, not licensed stock footage. The render is not considered publish-ready until the workflow verifies duration, audio stream, dimensions and file size.

## Important fact guardrails
- The S&P 500's 11.6% decline from its 6 October record is correction territory, not a confirmed bear market.
- The ECB's more-than-$4 trillion figure is off-balance-sheet commitments, not recognized debt.
- Circular financing is a vulnerability, not proof of fraud.
- No claim that AI is fake or that a crash is certain.
- Educational content only; not investment advice.
