# Visual and motion plan

## Creative direction

Minimal black editorial environment, ivory typography, restrained chart accents and clear numerical hierarchy. The episode is a data-led explainer, not a stock-footage montage. Every scene must animate a concept or reveal a relationship. Do not use static chapter cards to pad runtime.

**Planned format targets**
- Long-form: 1920×1080, 16:9, 6–10 minutes.
- Short: 1080×1920, 9:16, 45–90 seconds.

## Long-form scene plan

| Time | Scene | Visual action and camera/motion | Evidence / purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:45 | Three thermometers | Three vertical instrument columns rise at different rates; CPI, WPI and ICI labels lock into place. Camera pushes through the gap between them. | Establish the three numbers and the central question. |
| 00:45–01:35 | Three measurement layers | A shopping basket, a factory-gate conveyor and an eight-sector industrial ring assemble as three distinct animated systems. | Show that the indices measure different stages. |
| 01:35–02:35 | CPI household basket | Food, transport, housing and services icons orbit a weighted basket; food inflation becomes the highlighted component. Rural and urban readings appear side by side. | Explain the household-consumption lens and its limits. |
| 02:35–03:45 | WPI cost pipeline | Raw materials move through a refinery, power grid and factory line; cost bars build upstream, then a separate consumer-price meter appears downstream. | Explain wholesale costs and uncertain pass-through. |
| 03:45–04:55 | Eight-industry engine | An eight-part circular engine rotates; positive sectors extend outward while natural gas, crude oil and fertilizer bars move inward. | Show sector divergence inside the aggregate. |
| 04:55–06:10 | Market transmission | Animated chain: input costs → margins → cash flow → borrowing costs/bond yields → valuation. Branches show different outcomes by company. | Explain mechanisms without making deterministic predictions. |
| 06:10–07:05 | RBI policy corridor | A timeline marks the 7 Oct repo decision and the 2–4 Dec scheduled MPC meeting. The future node remains visibly unresolved. | Separate past policy from future uncertainty. |
| 07:05–07:50 | Four questions | Four rotating dashboard tiles reveal CPI components, WPI pass-through, industrial breadth and company margins. | Give viewers a practical analytical framework. |
| 07:50–08:10 | Closing synthesis | The three instruments settle into separate lanes that converge into one balanced macro dashboard. | Resolve the opening question. |

## Short-form storyboard

1. 0–5 sec: oversized hook — “उत्पादन वाढतंय. मग महागाई का?”
2. 5–17 sec: three animated meters reveal CPI 6.09%, WPI 12.29%, ICI 10.9%.
3. 17–31 sec: shopping basket, wholesale pipeline and eight-sector engine separate into three clear measurement layers.
4. 31–46 sec: one incorrect equivalence — “WPI 12.29% = दुकानातील प्रत्येक वस्तू 12.29% महाग?” — is crossed out, followed by the correct distinction.
5. 46–60 sec: cost → margin → rates → valuation path animates; concise disclaimer and source strip close the piece.

## Motion rules

- Use chart geometry driven only by `motion_graphics_data.json`.
- Every number on screen carries its period and provisional/final status.
- Use animated vector/3D objects and camera movement; no repeated static frames to extend duration.
- Use a native vertical composition for the Short; do not crop the horizontal master.
- No realistic AI-generated footage is planned. All visuals are explanatory abstractions, not evidence of a real event.
- No third-party footage, music or logos are included in this preproduction package.
