# Original visual and motion plan

**Style:** near-black editorial canvas, ivory typography, restrained signal amber; no stock footage or borrowed news photography. All graphics are original vector/3D assets. No music until a rights-cleared source is identified; use clean narration and original Foley only if created and documented.

## Long-form sequence
1. Tokyo business district as original low-poly/procedural 3D night-to-dawn establishing move; headline and data date.
2. Animated service-price index dial: 3.8% September revised to 4.2% October; label year-on-year.
3. Split-screen diagram distinguishing CPI household basket from SPPI business-to-business services.
4. Moving logistics network, leasing meter and hotel operations icons; show only cited contributor categories.
5. Cost pass-through flow: supplier -> business margin/productivity -> customer price. Animate three branches, no fabricated numerical values.
6. BOJ policy-rate timeline marker at 1.25% (September 2026) with explicit source/date; no forecast line.
7. Bond-price/yield inverse relationship with a mathematically correct illustrative curve, labelled conceptual.
8. Government budget / bridging-bond flow diagram based on draft guideline; visually distinguish government's stated accounting treatment from economic risk.
9. Yen-funded carry trade mechanism as conceptual arrows, labelled possible channel, not a current measured position.
10. Five investor questions and educational disclaimer.

## Short
Single narrative: headline figure -> what SPPI measures -> three cost examples -> possible pass-through -> BOJ uncertainty -> disclaimer. Each scene has purposeful kinetic typography and animated diagram movement; no sequence of static title cards.
