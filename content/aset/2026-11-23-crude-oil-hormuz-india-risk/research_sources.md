## Calculation and interpretation policy

- No forecast is made for Brent, WTI, the rupee or Indian pump prices.
- 20.9 million b/d, 20%, 25%, 89% and 74% are EIA historical estimates for 1H25. They are not presented as 23 Nov 2026 real-time traffic.
- 88.7% is a provisional FY2025-26 government figure, not a permanent structural constant.
- The 23 Nov oil-price numbers are Reuters' 02:16 GMT snapshot, not a market close.
- A lower dollar crude price can reduce the import-cost pressure, but INR/USD, cargo timing, grade differentials, freight, insurance and taxes also matter. No exact pass-through to retail prices is claimed.
- The rupee's weekly gain is not described as being caused by oil alone.
- OPEC+ meeting outcomes are unknown; the video only says the meeting is scheduled.
