# Research and fact-check notes — ASET 002
**Cut-off:** 22 November 2026, Asia/Kolkata. **Latest US close:** Friday 20 November 2026.

## Core claims and caveats
1. Reuters reports S&P 500 11.6% below its 6 October 2026 record after seven consecutive sessions of losses; Nasdaq erased 2026 gains. Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/correction-or-deeper-reckoning-investors-seek-signs-us-stock-selling-will-abate-2026-11-22/
2. Reuters reports global selloff, Taiwan equities down 17% in the week, almost $9 trillion erased from global stock values since August, VIX at 30 in its 20 November wrap. Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/global-markets-selloff-2026-11-20/
3. ECB Financial Stability Review (18 November 2026) says AI valuations depend on ambitious earnings expectations; AI infrastructure spending increasingly relies on external funding, including debt markets; a change in sentiment can spread losses across equities and bonds. Source: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/html/ecb.fsr202611~e03b919d36.en.html
4. ECB November 2026 review says most hyperscalers' free cash flow declined considerably since early 2025, turned negative in 2026 and is expected to remain so until 2028; funding reliance shifted toward external sources. Do not generalize this to every AI company. Source: same ECB report, section 2.3.
5. ECB estimates >USD4 trillion in Big Tech off-balance-sheet commitments as of Q2 2026, largely future contractual commitments related to AI infrastructure. Do not call this recognized debt or hidden debt. Source: ECB report, Box 3: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/html/ecb.fsr202611~e03b919d36.en.html
6. ECB describes interconnected financing across AI labs, cloud providers, chipmakers and infrastructure owners. This is a risk mechanism, not evidence of fraud. Source: ECB report, Box 3.
7. Market convention: a correction is generally a decline of at least 10% from a recent high; bear market typically 20% or more. Source: Fidelity investor education: https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/smart-money/stock-market-correction

## Editorial guardrails
- Do not forecast an inevitable crash or claim AI is fake.
- Do not conflate future commitments with recognized liabilities.
- Do not equate circular financing with fraud.
- State that AI can be transformative even if some investments fail.
- Avoid buy/sell/price-target instructions. Include educational-only disclaimer.
- Market data are dated snapshots, not live quotes.
- India implications are scenario pathways, not forecasts or verified market moves.
